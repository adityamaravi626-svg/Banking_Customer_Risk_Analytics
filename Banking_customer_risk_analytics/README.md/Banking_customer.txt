# Banking Customer Risk, Marketing & Loan Analytics

An end-to-end banking analytics project using Python, Statistics, Machine Learning, GenAI, SQL, and Power BI to analyze customer characteristics, loan-related indicators, marketing campaigns, and term-deposit subscription behavior.

---

## Project Overview

Banks conduct marketing campaigns to contact customers and encourage them to subscribe to financial products such as term deposits.

This project analyzes customer and campaign data to answer questions such as:

- Which customer groups are more likely to subscribe?
- Which jobs and education groups show higher subscription rates?
- How do housing loans, personal loans, and default status relate to subscription behavior?
- How does campaign contact intensity affect outcomes?
- Which contact methods and campaign periods perform better?
- How are economic indicators associated with customer response?
- Can Machine Learning estimate the probability of a customer subscribing?
- How can GenAI convert analytical and ML outputs into useful business insights?

The project combines data analytics, statistical analysis, machine learning, SQL, GenAI, and interactive Power BI dashboards into one complete workflow.

---

## Business Problem

The main objective is to understand customer response to bank marketing campaigns and identify patterns that can help improve campaign targeting.

The analysis focuses on:

1. Customer demographics
2. Loan and financial indicators
3. Marketing campaign performance
4. Previous campaign interactions
5. Economic conditions
6. Term-deposit subscription behavior
7. Machine Learning based subscription probability

---

## Dataset

The project uses the **Bank Marketing Dataset**.

### Important Target Variable

The target variable is:

`y`

It represents whether the customer subscribed to a **term deposit**.

| Value | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 0 / no | Customer did not subscribe |
| 1 / yes | Customer subscribed |

> **Important:** This dataset does not contain loan default as the prediction target. The `default`, `housing`, and `loan` columns are used as customer/loan-related indicators, while `y` represents term-deposit subscription.
